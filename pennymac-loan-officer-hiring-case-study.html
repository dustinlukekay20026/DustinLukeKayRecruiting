Mortgage recruiting case study

How Dustin Luke Kay Helped Scale Loan Officer Hiring from 300 to 900 at PennyMac

By Dustin Luke Kay | Luke Kay

As Vice President of Talent Acquisition at PennyMac, I led a team of seven recruiters during a period of significant mortgage hiring growth. Annual loan officer hiring grew from approximately 300 to 900, and average time to fill fell from roughly 36–42 days to 28 days.

The hiring challenge

Expanding loan officer hiring meant building capacity across the recruiting process. The team had to keep candidates moving through interviews and decisions while giving hiring leaders a clear picture of where hiring stood.

Where the recruiting team focused

Our work centered on workforce planning, recruiter productivity, and improving recruiting workflows. The two numbers that best describe the outcome are annual hires and average time to fill. Together they show how much capacity grew and how quickly roles were filled.

What recruiting leaders can apply

If you’re preparing for a similar increase in hiring, start by translating the annual target into a monthly plan and comparing it with your team’s capacity. Track hires and time to fill together. When roles slow down, look at where candidates are waiting, such as interview scheduling or hiring manager feedback, before deciding where the team needs more support.
